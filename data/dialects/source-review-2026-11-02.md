# Curated lexical source review — 2026-11-02

## Scope and evidence status

This review records exact-source research checked during this task, including
parallel Northern/Isan research and direct Southern source checks. Existing
project work is preserved. No dictionary corpus is bulk imported and no
หวังเหวิด entry is introduced without sufficient support.

Nine lexical notes are added: four Northern and five Isan. Each has
`provenance: editorial_context_note` and `only_modes: [own dialect, auto]` to
avoid unrelated slang-mode retrieval. The existing เว้า note receives an Esan108
corroborating citation and editorial provenance. These are short paraphrases and
contextual safeguards, not copied dictionary prose, automatic replacements,
whole-sentence hardcodes, model training or an accuracy benchmark. Explicit
personal definitions and clarification take precedence. Literal senses,
negation, questions, participants and relationships must remain intact.

## Attribution and reuse limits

- **มรดกล้านนา / พจนานุกรมล้านนา:** identify the dictionary and its named
  compiler **อ.จรีย์ สุนทรสิงห์**. Do not present these entries as university
  endorsement or independent university verification. The two searches used for
  an expression remain one dictionary, not two independent sources.
- **Esan108:** a dictionary site with mixed attribution. Do not assign every
  entry to ปรีชา พิณทอง (Preecha Phinthong) or imply one compiler verified every
  sense. Cite the exact entry and the limited fact or example it supports.
- **Copyright:** neither มรดกล้านนา nor Esan108 has an established open license
  in the supplied review. Treat both as copyrighted; links and concise original
  lexical/contextual paraphrases do not authorize bulk extraction or
  redistribution of dictionary text. No dictionary corpus is imported here.
- **Thai Wiktionary / วิกิพจนานุกรม contributors:** attribution is through each
  linked entry, with its contributors and revision history. Text is identified
  as **CC BY-SA 4.0**; license:
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>. The JSON citations retain
  `license: CC-BY-SA-4.0`. These notes paraphrase lexical facts; any reuse of
  protected source expression or adaptations must comply with attribution and
  share-alike obligations. This review does not relicense other dictionaries.
- **Longdo:** the อู้ contrast is specifically attributed to the Royal Institute
  dictionary entries displayed by Longdo, not to every result on its aggregated
  search page and not to Longdo as authority for the Northern speech sense.

## Northern evidence and verified URLs

### `northern-speak-uu` — อู้

Speech/talk sense:
- มรดกล้านนา: <https://moradoklanna.com/dict/?s=อู้>
- Wiktionary, Tai Tham headword ᩋᩪ᩶:
  <https://th.wiktionary.org/wiki/ᩋᩪ᩶>

Standard Thai contrasts, slacking off in อู้งาน and the instrument ซออู้:
- Longdo, Royal Institute entries: <https://dict.longdo.com/search/อู้>

The Northern sense has cross-source corroboration, but the Tai Tham entry is
not a separately verified Thai-script headword. Speech guidance must not erase
an explicit work-slacking or musical-instrument context.

### `northern-miss-kuet-tueng` — กึ๊ดเติง

Miss/think-of sense:
- <https://moradoklanna.com/dict/?s=กึ๊ดเติง&l=thai>

Related longer expression กึ๊ดเติงหาหน๋อกๆ:
- <https://moradoklanna.com/dict/?s=กึ๊ดเติงหา>

The second search supports a longer expression containing กึ๊ดเติงหา, **not an
independent headword for bare กึ๊ดเติงหา**. No second-dictionary corroboration
was supplied. The note therefore uses กึ๊ดเติง as its term, adds no unsupported
alias, and does not certify a whole-sentence translation or romantic intent.

### `northern-intensity-khanat` — ขนาด

Northern degree/intensifier use, including ลําขนาด:
- <https://moradoklanna.com/dict/?s=ขนาด>

## Isan evidence and verified URLs

### `isan-fatigue-mueai` — เมื่อย

Fatigue versus standard Thai aching/bodily discomfort:
- <https://th.wiktionary.org/wiki/เมื่อย>

The fatigue question example เมื่อยบ่:
- <https://esan108.com/dict/view/บ่>

The Esan108 evidence is an example under บ่, not an independently fetched
เมื่อย headword. It corroborates use in a fatigue question without establishing
a compulsory full-sentence translation, subject or diagnosis.

### `isan-negation-question-bo` — บ่

Negation versus final yes/no question, including เมื่อยบ่:
- <https://esan108.com/dict/view/บ่>

Negative-particle corroboration:
- <https://th.wiktionary.org/wiki/บ่>

The final-question example is credited to Esan108; the Wiktionary citation is
not claimed to verify every construction. Position is a contextual cue, not an
unconditional substitution of ไม่ or ไหม. A fatigue question must not silently
become an assertion that someone is not tired.

The lexical distinction has cross-source support. Keeping familial or general
love distinct from romance is editorial relationship-preservation guidance,

## Southern evidence and verified URLs

- **พันพรือ**: <https://dict.longdo.com/search/พันพรือ> supplies a Southern
  how-sense in an explicitly unapproved user entry. The component and spelling
  variants are supported by <https://th.wiktionary.org/wiki/ผรื่อ>. The full
  `พันพรือมั่ง` check-in and English wording remain editorial context guidance.
- **หรอย**: <https://th.wiktionary.org/wiki/หรอย> supports the taste sense;
  <https://dict.longdo.com/search/หรอย> has a Southern Thai–Thai section with
  taste/enjoyment senses. The note now distinguishes food from activities.
  Subtitle substring matches on the same page are not used as dialect evidence.
- **แหลง**: <https://th.wiktionary.org/wiki/แหลง> supports the speech verb;
  <https://dict.longdo.com/search/แหลง> corroborates only through an explicitly
  unapproved Southern user entry. No claim of independent scholarly validation.
- **หลบ**: <https://dict.longdo.com/search/หลบ> supplies Southern return through
  an explicitly unapproved user entry. Its Royal Institute section and
  <https://th.wiktionary.org/wiki/หลบ> document standard avoid/hide contrasts,
  not independent verification of the regional return-home construction.
  The new note therefore treats return as a contextual clue, never a forced
  replacement for hiding, shelter or avoidance.
- **แล**: <https://dict.longdo.com/search/แล> distinguishes look, conjunction
  and final-particle senses and has an unapproved ลองแล example. No broad
  single-character lexical entry was added: collisions with แลก/และ and other
  compounds would require more precise contextual retrieval.
- **หวังเหวิด / หวังเหว้อ**: the checked Longdo searches yielded no entries:
  <https://dict.longdo.com/search/หวังเหวิด> and
  <https://dict.longdo.com/search/หวังเหว้อ>. These failed lookups are recorded
  as evidence gaps, not proof the expressions are invalid or nonexistent.
  Prior model guesses are not treated as dictionary definitions.

## Behavioral scope

Explicit Isan requests with the new fatigue reference proceed to contextual
interpretation instead of unconditionally stopping on เมื่อย. Unknown-variety
Auto requests and explicit Isan requests without that reference retain the
fatigue/pain clarification fallback. Explicit pain still reaches the model
unchanged. Regression tests mock translations and check this routing, reference
selection, dialect isolation and literal-sense guidance; they do not measure
actual provider accuracy or prove that a model follows every note.

## Limitations

Cross-source corroboration here means that more than one cited publication
supports the lexical reading; it does not establish editorial independence of
all underlying dictionary material. Single-source readings and example-only
support are identified above. English choices, contextual exclusions and
personal-meaning precedence are application editorial guidance, not quotations
or dictionary-certified sentence translations. This review makes no broader
claim about dialect coverage, speaker consensus or real-model accuracy.
